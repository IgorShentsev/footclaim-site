Claim the city on foot.

Walk or run a closed loop. The ground inside becomes your zone on the map, with your name, a line of text and a link. Others can walk through it and take a piece back.

  1. Walk a loopRound a block, a park or a whole neighbourhood. Walking counts as much as running.
  2. Close itCome back to a point you already passed. The area inside is yours for 30 days.
  3. Defend itAnother player's loop through your zone takes the overlap. Walk it again to win it back and restart the clock.

Footclaim is in a closed beta on iPhone in Limassol, Cyprus. Questions or want to join: hello@footclaim.app
