Who can play

You must be 16 or older. One account per person.

The game

  • A closed loop you walk or run becomes your zone. Zones last 30 days; a new loop over your zone restarts the clock.
  • Another player's loop through your zone takes the overlapping part. Parts smaller than 1,000 m² or narrower than 25 m do not count.
  • We may change the game rules and numbers to keep it fair and fun, and we may reset or remove zones, for example after a bug or abuse. Zones have no monetary value and are not your property.
  • Footclaim is in beta. It may change, stop working for a while, or lose data.

Fair play

Zones must be made on foot. Cycling, driving, riding a scooter, GPS spoofing, modified apps and sharing accounts are not allowed. We check runs automatically and may reject runs, remove zones or close accounts that cheat.

Your safety comes first

Pay attention to traffic and your surroundings, follow traffic rules, and do not enter private property, closed areas or anywhere unsafe to claim a zone. Footclaim never asks you to go anywhere in particular. You take part at your own risk.
